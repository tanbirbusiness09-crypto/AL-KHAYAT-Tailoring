Who We Are

Al-Khayat Premium Tailoring

Located in the heart of Saudi Arabia, Al-Khayat Premium Tailoring has been the trusted name in bespoke traditional garments since 2015.

We specialize in Saudi, Emirati, Qatari, and Kuwaiti style thobes, jubbas, and saiderias. Using only the finest Japanese and Chinese fabrics.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

Home / Privacy

1. Information Collection

We collect personal information such as name, phone number, and measurements solely to provide bespoke tailoring services.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used to process orders, schedule home visits, and communicate project status via WhatsApp or phone.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption to protect your measurements and personal details from unauthorized access.

4. Third Parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party marketing agencies. Data is shared only with logistics partners for delivery.

Tailoring Methods

How Would You Like to Proceed?

Choose how you want to book your tailoring. We offer flexible options, and Delivery is FREE if you purchase fabric from us!

Home Visit

I Have My Own Fabric

Book online and our Muallim will visit your home to take measurements. We'll pick up your fabric, tailor it, and deliver the finished garment to your doorstep.

  • ✔️ Professional fitting at home
  • ✔️ Sew & Deliver back to you
  • 💰 Cost: Sewing Fee + Home Visit/Delivery Fee
VIP Service

Select Fabric via Catalog At Home

Our Muallim brings our premium fabric catalogs directly to your home. Touch, feel, and select your fabric. Get measured instantly.

  • ✔️ Premium catalogs delivered to you
  • ✔️ Measure & pick at home
  • 🎁 FREE Delivery / Visit Fee (since you buy fabric from us)
Online Order

Buy Online + Home Measurement

Select your fabric online directly from our site. You only pay for the fabric now. The Muallim visits your home to measure, and you pay the sewing fee later!

  • ✔️ Select fabric from website
  • ✔️ Pay for fabric only at checkout
  • 🎁 FREE Delivery / Visit Fee
